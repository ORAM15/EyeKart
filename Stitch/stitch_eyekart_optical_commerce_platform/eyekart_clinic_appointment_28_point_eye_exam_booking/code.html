The 28 Diagnostic Evaluation Points Explained

Unlike ordinary 5-minute lens tests, EyeKart's clinical examination systematically audits every refractive refractive vector, neurological muscle balance, and cellular retinal health across four discrete pillars.

01 Points 1 – 7

Refractive & Visual Acuity

Eliminates aberrations, halos, and visual stress under day and twilight lighting.

  1. 1. Monocular Snellen & LogMAR High-Contrast Acuity
  2. 2. Dual Laser Wavefront Aberrometry (Zernike Polynomials)
  3. 3. Jackson Cross-Cylinder Astigmatism Axis Fine-Tuning
  4. 4. Binocular Red-Green Duochrome Equi-Balance
  5. 5. Dynamic Accommodation Amplitude & Facility
  6. 6. Near Addition & Working Distance Optimization
  7. 7. Contrast Sensitivity & Glare Recovery Threshold
Instrument: ZEISS VISUPHOR 500
02 Points 8 – 14

Binocular Motor & Alignment

Assesses ocular muscle coordination, tracking accuracy, and 3D depth perception.

  1. 8. Unilateral & Alternating Cover-Uncover Alignment Test
  2. 9. Hirschberg 1st Purkinje Corneal Light Reflection
  3. 10. Near Point of Convergence (NPC Break & Recovery)
  4. 11. Stereopsis Depth Perception (Randot Micro-Arcsec)
  5. 12. Smooth Pursuit & Saccadic Eye Tracking Mobility
  6. 13. Maddox Rod Horizontal & Vertical Phoria Prism
  7. 14. Fusional Vergence Positive / Negative Reserves
Instrument: Bernell Prism Diagnostic Bar
03 Points 15 – 21

Anterior Segment & Surface

Slit-lamp examination of cornea, eyelids, tear quality, and cataracts.

  1. 15. High-Magnification LED Slit-Lamp Biomicroscopy
  2. 16. Tear Film Break-Up Time (TBUT) Sodium Fluorescein
  3. 17. Meibomian Gland Infrared Surface Meibography
  4. 18. Corneal Endothelium Clarity & Striae Evaluation
  5. 19. Bulbar & Palpebral Conjunctival Vascular Grading
  6. 20. Van Herick Anterior Chamber Angle Glaucoma Check
  7. 21. Crystalline Lens Nuclear & Cortical Opacity Staging
Instrument: Haag-Streit BQ 900 Slit Lamp
04 Points 22 – 28

Posterior Retina & Glaucoma

Deep optical scanning for optic nerve cupping, macula, and blood pressure signs.

  1. 22. Non-Contact Rebound Intraocular Tonometry (IOP mmHg)
  2. 23. Cup-to-Disc (C/D) Ratio Optic Nerve Head Neuro-Scan
  3. 24. High-Definition Digital Retinal Fundus Photography
  4. 25. Macula Lutea & Foveal Avascular Reflex Inspection
  5. 26. Arteriovenous (A/V) Caliber & Vascular Tortuosity
  6. 27. Equatorial & Peripheral Retinal Sweep (Tears/Holes)
  7. 28. Corneal Thickness Biometry (Pachymetry Calibration)
Instrument: ZEISS Clarus 500 Ultra-Widefield
