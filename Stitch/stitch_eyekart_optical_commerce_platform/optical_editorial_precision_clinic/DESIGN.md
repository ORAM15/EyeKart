## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end optical craftsmanship merged with clinical clinical precision. Tailored for the discerning urban eyewear consumer in Nairobi and the wider East African market, the aesthetic unites the tactile authority of an atelier with the scientific rigor of clinical optometry.

The design philosophy combines **Modern Editorial Minimalism** and **Precision Glassmorphic Depth**:
- **Editorial Poise**: Restrained, architectural grid layouts with generous negative space, high-contrast serif typography for hero editorial narratives, and understated luxury detailing.
- **Clinical Precision**: Uncompromising legibility for prescription diagnostics, optical measurement tables (SPH, CYL, AXIS, PD), refractive index selectors, and localized M-PESA monetary flows.
- **Translucent Optics**: Controlled translucent glassmorphism that mirrors optical lenses—anti-reflective coatings, frosted prism planes, sub-pixel borders, and subtle refractive light passes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of optical purity and warm architectural luxury.

- **Primary (`#111418`)**: Deep Refined Ink. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks with an opulent, carbon-pigmented ink used for high-impact typography, primary action buttons, structural boundaries, and deep brand contrast.
- **Secondary (`#0E7490`)**: Clinical Deep Cyan / Teal. Evokes medical laser accuracy, anti-reflective lens coatings, and specialized optical instrumentation. Used for selected states, interactive focal points, and step-completion milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Warm Amber. References tortoiseshell frames, artisanal brass frame rivets, and golden hour lighting across Nairobi’s landscape. Deployed sparingly for luxury badges, limited editions, and VIP loyalty accents.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Titanium Slate. Represents lightweight frame materials (titanium, acetate core wires) and clinical balance. Powers secondary labeling, measurement units, and structural divider lines.
- **Specialized Roles**:
  - `surface-ivory` (`#FBFBFA`) and `surface-cream` (`#F4F3F0`): Foundational warm canvas layers that eliminate eye fatigue caused by harsh digital whites.
  - `optical-white` (`#FFFFFF`): Reserved exclusively for product display pads, lens clarity simulations, floating modal sheets, and active cards.
  - `mpesa-green` (`#00A859`): Reserved strictly for M-PESA checkout, instant STK-push confirmations, and transaction validation badges.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical high-fashion editorial grace with ultra-calibrated legibility.

- **Display & Large Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Used in brand-driven lifestyle spots, collection inaugurations, bespoke frame craft narratives, and luxury categorization titles. Brings distinct warmth and elegance to high-end eyewear.
- **Interface & Optometry (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Built with geometric balance and wide apertures, this family handles all functional UI, technical frame geometries (e.g., `51 □ 19 - 145`), and optometric matrices (SPH, CYL, AXIS, PD, ADD).
- **Currencies & Prescriptions**: Currency expressions (`KSh 18,500`) and prescription values use tabular lining numbers (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`) within `Plus Jakarta Sans` to maintain vertical alignment and immediate scanability across comparison matrices and invoices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by architectural symmetry and intentional breathing room.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile`: `< 768px` (4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`).
  - `Tablet`: `768px – 1024px` (8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`).
  - `Desktop`: `> 1024px` (12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`).
- **Rhythm Philosophy**: Optical products rely on clean framing. Product showcase grids alternate between tight technical specification pairs (`space-sm` / `space-md`) and expansive luxury editorial showcases (`space-xl`).
- **Diagnostic Form Modules**: Prescription entry cards use uniform 2-column (single eye OD/OS) or 6-column (SPH, CYL, AXIS, ADD, PD) micro-grids with strictly enforced `space-sm` field gaps to prevent entry errors during eye test uploads.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical optical properties: light diffusion, translucent refractions, and sub-pixel edge boundaries. Heavy dropped shadows are avoided in favor of clinical atmospheric tiers.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas)**: Background flat tone (`#FBFBFA` or `#F4F3F0`). Pure planar stability.
- **Level 1 (Product Trays & Cards)**: Elevated by a micro-border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-opacity shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -6px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Optical Selectors)**: Dynamic lift for interactive frames. Shadow deepens smoothly: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Virtual Try-On / 3D Viewer Overlays & Floating Sheets)**: Translucent glassmorphic scrim: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle interior highlight border `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.
- **Level 4 (Global Modals & Prescription Previewers)**: Grounded dark atmospheric drop: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses **Soft Roundedness** (`roundedness: 1`), conveying the calibrated precision of optical instruments and hand-cut lenses rather than generic bubbly forms.

- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Applied to clinical data tables, prescription numeric steppers, and pill tags.
- **Cards, Frames, & Diagnostic Containers (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Frame preview modules, measurement panels, and virtual try-on viewports use 8px corners to evoke beveled glass edges.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges**: Main CTAs and badges use controlled 4px to 6px radii. Lens coating circular chips (e.g., Blue-Blocker, Anti-Glare, Photochromic) remain true circles (`50%` / circular).

## Components

### Buttons & Primary Actions
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#111418`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, 48px standard height, `label-lg` styling. On hover, transitions to `#2A2E35` with subtle elevation.
- **Secondary Prescription Action**: 1px border `#111418`, background transparent, text `#111418`. Hover fills with `rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.04)`.
- **M-PESA Express Button**: Background `#00A859`, text `#FFFFFF`, high-emphasis font weight, displaying the official stylized M-PESA indicator alongside localized KSh totals.

### Prescription Matrix & Lens Customizer
- **OD / OS Clinical Grid**: Segmented into Right (OD) and Left (OS) ocular lanes. Dropdowns and manual inputs for SPH, CYL, AXIS, and ADD are encased in `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` borders, transitioning to `#0E7490` ring boundaries on active focus.
- **Pupillary Distance (PD) Dual Ruler**: Precision slider with numerical readout in millimetres (`data-metric`), accompanied by visual pupil targeting guides.
- **Step Indicators**: Linear rail with numbered nodes. Completed stages highlight in `#0E7490`, active stage in `#111418`, pending stages in `#E2E8F0`.

### Virtual Try-On (VTO) & 3D Interactive Chrome
- **Camera Frame HUD**: Frosted glassmorphic HUD pill dock anchored to the bottom screen edge (`background: rgba(17, 20, 24, 0.7)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Targeting Reticle**: Ultra-fine `#06B6D4` vector crosshairs that lock onto the bridge of the nose and temple widths, providing immediate millimetre feedback.
- **3D Rotation Tooltip**: Micro-pill badge hovering dynamically over the spectacle silhouette with an integrated 360-degree rotation icon.

### Eyewear Product Cards
- **Product Stage**: `#FFFFFF` pedestal container bounded by `#E2E8F0`, with top-right amber badge (`#D97706`) for limited releases or handcrafted acetate flags.
- **Frame Spec Ribbon**: Discrete subtitle beneath the frame name detailing frame dimensions (`Lens: 52mm | Bridge: 18mm | Temple: 140mm`) in `#64748B`.
- **Color Swatch Selector**: Circular swatches (`16px`) surrounded by a hairline whitespace ring and selected state indicator.
